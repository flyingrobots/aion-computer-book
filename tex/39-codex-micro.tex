\noindent
\textbf{Core dictionary (20 seconds to re-orient yourself):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{State} $\equiv G$ \emph{(RMG)}. Everything lives on a graph-of-graphs.
  \item \textbf{Law} $\equiv \mathcal{R}$ \emph{(DPO rules)}. All dynamics is local rewrite.
  \item \textbf{Universe} $\equiv (G,\mathcal{R})$ plus causal graph of events.
  \item \textbf{MRMW} $\equiv$ the space of all such universes and their histories.
  \item \textbf{Multiversal machine} $M$ acts on \emph{distributions} over MRMW, not single runs.
  \item \textbf{Geometry} is how distances, geodesics, curvature, and measures behave on MRMW.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
If you remember nothing else: think in terms of $(G,\mathcal{R})$, causal graphs,
coarse-grainings $C$, and machines $M$ that move \emph{measures} over MRMW around.
Everything in the main text is just those four ideas dressed up.
